## Ranked 3 Kings Bets

1. [CTR/Ranking] `/blog/fixing-pixelated-photos`

   Evidence: 1,894 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 11.0 for `how to fix pixelated photos`. Current H1 is too broad: `How to Fix Pixelated Photos: An Educational Guide to AI Upscaling`.

   Recommended Three Kings:

   - Title: `How to Fix Pixelated Photos Online with AI (Free, 2026)`
   - H1: `How to Fix Pixelated Photos with AI`
   - First paragraph: answer immediately: `To fix pixelated photos, upscale the image first, then reduce blocky edges with AI sharpening or deblurring...`

   Supporting improvement: add a first-screen table with `Cause / Best fix / Tool path`.

   SERP intent: practical how-to. Competitors include Adobe's Photoshop guide and older editor workflow pages such as Techwalla.

2. [CTR/Ranking] `/blog/topaz-video-upscaler`

   Evidence: 6,210 impressions, 2 clicks, 0.03% CTR, average position 9.34. GA engagement is good, so traffic quality is likely acceptable.

   Recommended Three Kings:

   - Title: `Topaz Video AI Alternatives 2026: Price, Features & Free Options`
   - H1: `Topaz Video AI 2026: Features, Price, and Alternatives`
   - First paragraph: lead with the comparison promise for people deciding whether Topaz Video AI is worth it in 2026.

   Supporting improvement: add or raise a comparison table near the top: Topaz vs free/cloud/lifetime-license alternatives.

   SERP intent: comparison-heavy, with ranking pages around best AI video upscalers and Topaz alternatives.

3. [Ranking/Consolidation] `/blog/how-ai-image-upscaling-works-guide`

   Evidence: 295 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 13.77 for `ai image upscaling explained`; cannibalized by `/blog/how-ai-image-upscaling-works-explained`.

   Recommended action: consolidate or clearly differentiate before copy work. Do not split `how AI image upscaling works` across two near-identical URLs.

4. [CTR] `/blog/topaz-labs-free-trial`

   Evidence: grouped trial queries around positions 10-12 with low clicks, plus GA bounce rate of 52.6%.

   Recommended Three Kings:

   - Title: `Topaz Labs Free Trial 2026: What You Get and How to Start`
   - H1: `Topaz Labs Free Trial: Photo AI and Video AI Trial Guide`
   - First paragraph: answer `Does Topaz Labs have a free trial in 2026?` immediately.

   Supporting improvement: fix the currently truncated meta description.

5. [CTR] `/alternatives/vs-adobe-express`

   Evidence: `adobe express image upscaler` has 276 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 7.42.

   Recommended Three Kings:
