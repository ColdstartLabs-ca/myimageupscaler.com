## Context

GSC organic clicks fell to 6,200 (Aug 17–Sep 13) from 7,523 (Jul 20–Aug 16), −17.6%; the homepage lost 941 clicks and the flagship lost 666, partly offset by gainers, so no sitewide collapse is established. The latest GSC export ends September 13. Local Lighthouse 12.8.2 mobile median is 76 with simulated LCP 4.54s; two cold samples show TTFB at 81–86% of observed LCP while the hero image loaded in 28–119ms. The PageSpeed API returned a 429, so no Google-hosted score exists, and this is lab evidence — it does not prove performance caused the search decline. Field review of the 114 affected URLs (rolling validation started September 8) is due around October 6.

The business outcome is qualified organic visitors, not payment conversion. Revenue owns paid-event and attribution definitions; this PRD consumes verified paid attribution only when available and otherwise stays independent.
